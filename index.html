About us

Bridging hospital care and the comfort of home

Lenorplus Home Health and Bedside Care is a healthcare service provider committed to delivering accessible, compassionate, professional and patient-centred healthcare services.

We aim to improve the quality of life of individuals and families through home-based healthcare, bedside nursing, mental health services, rehabilitation, counselling, medical support and community-based interventions.

Our approach recognises that healthcare extends beyond hospitals. Many individuals require continuous medical attention, psychological support, rehabilitation and assistance with daily living even after leaving healthcare facilities.

Lenorplus therefore seeks to bridge the gap between hospital-based treatment and recovery at home and within the community. We work towards promoting physical health, mental wellbeing, functional independence, recovery and social reintegration.

Our services

Comprehensive care, wherever you are

From hospital-to-home transition to long-term rehabilitation, our multidisciplinary team delivers dignified, professional support across Uganda.

Mental health & psychiatric services

Supporting individuals experiencing psychological, emotional and psychiatric challenges — including home-based psychiatric care and structured inpatient rehabilitation.

Home & inpatient

Home-based psychiatric care

Continued mental health support after hospital discharge or for conditions manageable in the community. Ideal for clinically stable patients who thrive at home.

Inpatient mental health rehabilitation

Structured residential rehabilitation for individuals needing continuous supervision, treatment support and functional recovery — preparing them to return to family and community.

Drug & substance use rehabilitation

Professional assessment, structured treatment and long-term rehabilitation for alcohol and drug dependence — compassionate, evidence-based care.

Psychiatric emergency services

Prompt response for psychiatric emergencies with initial assessment, stabilisation, and safe referral or transfer arrangements — protecting patients and those around them.

Elderly care services

Personal and healthcare support for seniors facing ageing, chronic illness or reduced mobility — promoting dignity, safety and quality of life at home.

Bedside nursing & home healthcare

Professional nursing at home, hospital or other settings — cancer, stroke, diabetes, post-surgical care, pre/post-natal care, wound dressing, physiotherapy and orthopedics across Uganda.
